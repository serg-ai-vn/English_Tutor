# Pattern Bank v2 — 20 Live Spoken C1 Patterns

Заменяет Super-Bank of 40 (глава 6 research_materials.md) от 2026-08-25. Причина: старый банк собран из Manchester Academic Phrasebook — язык научных статей, не разговорной речи. Новый банк: живые устные C1-паттерны для ежедневной коммуникации и питча. Директор центра — скорее всего non-native, язык должен быть ясным и естественным.

## Принципы

1. **Каждый паттерн = готовое предложение**, не абстрактный шаблон с [A]/[B]. Front карточки — полный питч-пример с артиклями. Структура — в back как справка.
2. **Два контекста**: pitch (продажа курса) + daily (обычная жизнь). Ученик видит, что паттерн работает везде, не только в питче.
3. **Живой регистр**: разговорный, не академический. «The data clearly shows a strong link» вместо «The empirical evidence points toward a robust correlation».
4. **Секция питча**: каждый паттерн привязан к секции (hook / problem / solution / evidence / ask) — карточки собираются в линию питча.
5. **20 паттернов, не 40**: качество важнее количества. Распределение по функциям намеренно неравномерное: 5 hedging, 4 emphasis, 4 cause-effect, 4 data, 3 signposting.

## Cat 1: Hedging & Softening (5 паттернов)

Для вежливого несогласия, смягчения утверждений, работы с возражениями. Живой разговорный тон.

### S1 — Гипотетический вопрос (id 56)

**Структура:** Let's say we could [X] — what would that mean for your [Y]?

**Pitch:** Let's say we could cut your teachers' grading time in half — what would that mean for your center's schedule?

**Daily:** Let's say we could leave an hour earlier — what would you do with the time?

**Секция:** solution / ask | **Регистр:** spoken

### S2 — Валидация перед pivot (id 1)

**Структура:** I totally get why you'd think [X]. But actually, [Y].

**Pitch:** I totally get why you'd worry about kids spending more time on screens. But actually, the platform frees up teachers for more face-to-face activities.

**Daily:** I get why you'd think it's too expensive. But actually, it pays for itself in two months.

**Секция:** problem / solution | **Регистр:** spoken

### S3 — Рефрейминг негатива (id 3)

**Структура:** Instead of seeing [X] as [negative], it's more useful to think of it as [positive].

**Pitch:** Instead of seeing AI as a replacement for your teachers, it's more useful to think of it as a teaching assistant that never gets tired.

**Daily:** Instead of seeing the new schedule as a problem, think of it as a chance to reorganize.

**Секция:** problem / solution | **Регистр:** spoken

### S4 — Ответ на concern с evidence (id 4)

**Структура:** That's a fair concern. Let me show you what happened when [someone] tried this.

**Pitch:** That's a fair concern about the cost. Let me show you what happened when three centers in Hanoi adopted this last year.

**Daily:** That's a fair concern. Let me show you what happened when my colleague tried the same approach.

**Секция:** evidence | **Регистр:** spoken

### S5 — Смягчение утверждения (id 2)

**Структура:** What we tend to see is that [Subject] [Verb] when [Condition].

**Pitch:** What we tend to see is that students progress faster when the platform adapts to their pace.

**Daily:** What we tend to see is that people stick with it when they see quick results.

**Секция:** evidence | **Регистр:** spoken

## Cat 2: Emphasis & Highlighting (4 паттерна)

Clefts и inversion для акцента на ключевом. Звучит естественно в устной речи.

### S6 — It-cleft для акцента (id 6)

**Структура:** What really makes this work is [NP].

**Pitch:** What really makes this work is the adaptive algorithm that pinpoints each student's weak spots.

**Daily:** What really makes this work is the simplicity — anyone can pick it up in a day.

**Секция:** solution | **Регистр:** spoken

### S7 — What-cleft для дифференциации (id 7)

**Структура:** What sets us apart from the usual approach is [NP].

**Pitch:** What sets us apart from the usual approach is that students get instant feedback on every sentence they speak.

**Daily:** What sets this place apart from the others is the chef actually comes to your table.

**Секция:** solution | **Регистр:** spoken

### S8 — Dual benefit (id 8)

**Структура:** Not only does it [X], but it also [Y].

**Pitch:** Not only does it reduce your teachers' workload, but it also gives parents real-time updates on their child's progress.

**Daily:** Not only does it save time, but it also cuts costs by about thirty percent.

**Секция:** solution / evidence | **Регистр:** spoken

### S9 — Редкость результата (id 9)

**Структура:** You rarely see a [NP] that actually [Verb].

**Pitch:** You rarely see a platform that actually keeps kids engaged for more than two weeks.

**Daily:** You rarely see a manager who actually listens to feedback like that.

**Секция:** solution | **Регистр:** spoken

## Cat 3: Cause-Effect & Reasoning (4 паттерна)

Связи причины и следствия. «Thereby» — единственный формальный элемент, но в устной речи работает.

### S10 — Прямое следствие (id 11)

**Структура:** [Main clause], thereby [gerund] [object].

**Pitch:** The platform adjusts difficulty in real time, thereby keeping every student in their optimal learning zone.

**Daily:** We automated the reports, thereby saving each teacher about three hours a week.

**Секция:** evidence | **Регистр:** spoken (formal but natural)

### S11 — Объяснение причины (id 12)

**Структура:** The main reason is simply that [Clause].

**Pitch:** The main reason is simply that students practice more when the content feels relevant to their lives.

**Daily:** The main reason is simply that people don't know the rules, not that they don't care.

**Секция:** evidence | **Регистр:** spoken

### S12 — Стратегическое выравнивание (id 14)

**Структура:** By combining [X] with [Y], we directly address [problem].

**Pitch:** By combining AI conversation practice with teacher-led activities, we directly address the shortage of qualified English teachers.

**Daily:** By combining exercise with diet changes, you directly address the root cause, not just the symptoms.

**Секция:** solution | **Регистр:** spoken

### S13 — Ответственность (id 10)

**Структура:** So it's really up to [person] like you to [action] before [consequence].

**Pitch:** So it's really up to directors like you to adopt this before your competitors do.

**Daily:** So it's up to you to set the tone before the team loses motivation.

**Секция:** ask | **Регистр:** spoken

## Cat 4: Data & Evidence (4 паттерна)

Ссылки на цифры и факты без академического языка. Ясно, конкретно, убедительно.

### S14 — Привлечение внимания к цифре (id 54)

**Структура:** The number that really stands out is [fact].

**Pitch:** The number that really stands out is that Vietnam needs 22,000 more English teachers by 2030.

**Daily:** The number that really stands out is that 80% of users come back within the first week.

**Секция:** evidence / hook | **Регистр:** spoken

### S15 — Связь двух фактов (id 15)

**Структура:** The data clearly shows a strong link between [A] and [B].

**Pitch:** The data clearly shows a strong link between Decision 2371 and the surge in demand for English teachers.

**Daily:** The data clearly shows a strong link between sleep quality and productivity at work.

**Секция:** evidence | **Регистр:** spoken

### S16 — Синтез вывода (id 16)

**Структура:** When you put it all together, the numbers clearly show that [Clause].

**Pitch:** When you put it all together, the numbers clearly show that centers using AI tools fill their classes faster than those that don't.

**Daily:** When you put it all together, the numbers clearly show that remote work isn't going away.

**Секция:** evidence | **Регистр:** spoken

### S17 — Результаты пилота (id 17)

**Структура:** Our first trial showed a clear improvement in [area].

**Pitch:** Our first trial showed a clear improvement in speaking confidence — students went from avoiding English to volunteering in class.

**Daily:** Our first attempt showed a clear improvement in response time.

**Секция:** evidence | **Регистр:** spoken

## Cat 5: Structuring & Signposting (3 паттерна)

Маркеры структуры для длинной речи — переходы, возражения, закрытие. Живые, не лекционные.

### S18 — Переход к практике (id 19)

**Структура:** Now that we've covered the theory, let me show you how it actually works.

**Pitch:** Now that we've covered the theory, let me show you how it actually works in a real classroom.

**Daily:** Now that we've covered the basics, let me show you how it actually works in practice.

**Секция:** solution | **Регистр:** spoken

### S19 — Отбивание возражения (id 5)

**Структура:** The main objection assumes [X], but that doesn't account for [Y].

**Pitch:** The main objection assumes you need technical staff to run this, but that doesn't account for the automated setup — it runs itself.

**Daily:** The main objection assumes it takes months, but that doesn't account for the streamlined onboarding.

**Секция:** problem / solution | **Регистр:** spoken

### S20 — Закрытие и призыв (id 20)

**Структура:** So to wrap up — bringing this in puts your center in a position to [Verb].

**Pitch:** So to wrap up — bringing this in puts your center in a position to offer something no competitor in your district currently has.

**Daily:** So to wrap up — making this change puts you in a position to attract better talent.

**Секция:** ask / close | **Регистр:** spoken
